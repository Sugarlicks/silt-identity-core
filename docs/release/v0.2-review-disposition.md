## Findings and disposition

### MINOR — LCP mapping basis line

**Finding:** The reviewer recalled that an earlier version of the experimental SILT ↔ LCP mapping cited RC2 and asked that the current mapping basis be confirmed.

**Disposition:** **CLOSED — already corrected before this review.**

The current mapping states:

> `Canonical SILT basis: SILT Core v0.2 Semantic Architecture, Freeze Candidate 1 (FC1), derived from RC4.1 pre-freeze stress-test revision.`

It also records that the mapping was rechecked against RC4.1/FC1 after completion of the pre-freeze worked encounters.

No change required.

### MINOR — §3 Participant example asymmetry

**Finding:** The Participant example list named `DAO` specifically alongside otherwise generic categories, creating a mild technical/organisational asymmetry in a section intended to remain ontologically neutral.

**Disposition:** **ACCEPTED — editorial correction applied.**

`DAO` has been removed from the §3 example list. The generic categories and open catch-all remain sufficient. No semantic meaning changes.

### MINOR — §10 / §11 Technical Capability and Authority cross-reference

**Finding:** §10 states the general Technical Capability ≠ Authority boundary, while §11 applies the distinction to cryptographic capability, credential and technical authority artefacts without explicitly cross-referencing §10.

**Disposition:** **ACCEPTED — editorial clarification applied.**

§11 now states:

> `Consistent with §10, possession or technical validity of such an artefact does not by itself establish SILT Authority.`

This clarifies an existing distinction and does not alter the architecture.

### FUTURE — correctly scoped outside v0.2

- KERI/ACDC, UCAN, zcap and other downstream implementation bindings;

- full Governance & IPR / SEP / trademark framework;

No action required for v0.2 freeze.

## Adversarial checks the reviewer reports as holding

The reviewer reported no release-blocking defect in the following areas:

- **Accidental ontology capture:** §§1–9 and 22–23 preserve the distinction between the receiving order's conditions and the legitimacy or ontology of the originating order.
- **Plural Profile Expressions:** §9 and WE03 preserve separate Profile-Expression-specific evaluation and do not silently merge or rank conflicting expressions.
- **Release claims:** the draft release notes and CHANGELOG remain aligned with the canonical Core and do not overstate what the pre-freeze tests establish.
- **WE01–WE04 consistency:** no contradiction was identified between the canonical architecture and the pressure-test suite in the reviewed material.
- **Holder question:** WE01 now supports treatment of holder-related position as domain-defined Standing rather than a universal Core role.
